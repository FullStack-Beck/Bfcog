Our Faith

What We Believe

Our Mission

Church of God Ministries serves the General Assembly by empowering the people of God to collectively build a Jesus-centered, biblically grounded Movement that brings the kingdom to life on earth as it is in heaven.

Matthew 6:10

Our Vision

Every day in our thoughts, words, and actions, we aspire to follow His example: Loving like Jesus loved, serving like Jesus served, giving like Jesus gave.

We are Jesus People

What We Hold To

Theological Commitments

Although the Church of God honors theological freedom within the bounds of biblically-based belief, affiliated churches are expected to hold persuasions that are in general agreement with the teaching tradition of the Church of God.

Sin & Salvation

We are Wesleyan Holiness Arminian in our understanding that mankind has been endowed with free will and is responsible to participate in choosing salvation. We proclaim that Jesus died for everyone and that anyone who repents and accepts the sacrifice of Christ on the cross for the forgiveness of their sins is saved. We further believe that since we have free will to accept or reject salvation, our free will also allows us to choose to disobey and reject God’s will, thereby forfeiting salvation.

Holiness

As Wesleyan Holiness Arminians with free(d) will, we are called to live our lives as a reflection of the Holy God who created us, the sinless Savior that died for us, and the Holy Spirit that infills us. We believe that holiness is lived out daily and is relational and therefore informs and impacts how we treat every person. This theological commitment addresses the power to live with victory over sin. We are not still prisoners to a sinful nature, but by the strength of the Holy Spirit in our life we are able to overcome temptation and sin.

Trinitarian Theology

Trinitarian theology strikes at the most foundational question of religion: “Who is God?” The doctrine of the Trinity means that there is one God who eternally exists as three distinct Persons: the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Stated differently, God is one in essence and three in person. These definitions express three crucial truths: The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are distinct Persons. Each Person is fully God. There is only one God.

Authority of Scripture

The Church of God holds to the teaching that all of the Bible’s 66 books are writings by authors who were inspired by God and that as such the Bible is the authoritative word of God that guides our daily lives and gives us understanding and godly wisdom. While some would suggest that these 66 books are simply a collection of stories of people who loved God and served Him and their interpretation of their God journey, we would declare that the messages of the Bible are inspired and trustworthy for guiding our lives.

Imago Dei

The creation story unfolds with such a powerful display of God’s forethought in all that God created. But in one final act of sovereignty and creation the triune Godhead declares there will be one more distinct living being: “humankind, created in our image!” From creation forward, God has had a different relationship, connection, and role with this “Imago Dei” than with any other part of His creation. Grasping this theological understanding and being committed to the implications of this truth changes how we see every other person!

The Kingdom of God

The Kingdom of God is probably one of the most common themes discussed and preached, and one of the least truly understood or lived out fully. The Church of God has an Amillennial view of the Kingdom of God. The King has already won the battle, on Calvary and over death and the grave, and therefore, the Kingdom is reigning here and now, and as such, Kingdom citizens fully have the power to live victoriously in Christ daily. We do not teach a future battle or that Christ will set up a throne on earth for a certain number of years. Rather, Jesus has won the battle, and His throne is in the heart of those who receive His salvation and follow Him in discipleship.

Unity

We are called by God to live in unity with our brothers and sisters in Christ. Not culture, nor ethnicity, gender, or age should separate us or prevent us from being fully embraced by one another and allowing every person to fully utilize the gifts and callings that God has invested in them.

Our Tradition

Church of God Distinctives

Church of God Distinctives are not equal to our Theological Commitments. However, they are an important part of the tradition, history, and culture within the movement.

Communion

Congregations in the Church of God practice communion in a variety of ways and various times. However, in the Church of God we do teach that the bread and cup are symbols of Christ’s body and blood, and we share communion to remember His sacrifice for us on Calvary and to look forward to His coming again to receive us into heaven.

Women in Ministry

The Church of God has embraced the truth that God is no respecter of persons and that He calls and gifts each of us according to His will, irrespective of gender, ethnicity, or age. We believe that the gifts of the Holy Spirit are active and in place in the body of Christ and that women as well as men may experience the calling and gifting of God for any position or role in the church.

Baby Dedication

We do not baptize babies because we practice “believer” baptism. However, we do invite and encourage parents to bring their children to the altar and dedicate themselves to raising their children to know, love and serve God by how they live out Christ in the home and how they teach their children.

Baptism

The Church of God teaches that baptism is the outward expression of the inward works of salvation. Baptism is not required for salvation but is the witness that we have died to sin and have been raised to new life in Christ.

Membership

We do not have formal membership but believe that salvation makes each person a member of God’s family.

Footwashing

The ordinance of foot washing has been practiced by the church since it was first instituted by Christ. The ordinance of feet washing reenacts the servant example that Christ established during the Passover meal in John 13:1-17. Each ordinance helps us remember that we are part of the family of Christ and helps us deepen our connection with all those who have come before us and those who will follow after us. The Church of God recognizes three ordinances: communion, baptism, and foot washing.

Gifts of the Spirit

We believe the gifts of the Holy Spirit continue to be given to each person for the edification of the body of Christ. These gifts are not for the advancement of a person, but rather for the church to be effective in her mission and ministry. The Church of God historically has interpreted the gift of tongues as God giving an individual the ability to understand or speak another language for the purpose of furthering the gospel, or as a private prayer language used for personal edification.

Marriage & Purity

The Church of God continues to define marriage as a union between one man and one woman intended to remain until death parts them. Sexual purity includes recognizing that our bodies are the temple of God, and that all people have been created in the image of God, and therefore, our relationships in thought and action ought to reflect those truths. It is God’s will that all people experience wholeness and live lives of holiness. This includes the stewardship and management of our sexuality within the Lord’s boundaries of reserving sexual union for marriage and practicing chastity when not in that covenantal relationship.

Sexual & Gender Identity

The Church of God holds that there are two genders, male and female. Homosexuality and other LBGTQ alternative relationships are not recognized as biblically acceptable. However, the church is still on mission to respond with love, compassion, grace, mercy, and truth to all people. This distinctive is perhaps the most difficult to address. The widespread change in laws and acceptance has now created a challenge for the Church of God and other Wesleyan Holiness faith groups to discern how to reach with love and teach with truth.

Amillennialism

The Church of God has historically stood firmly in the Amillennial camp, believing that Jesus established His Kingdom in the hearts of men and women when He came the first time (John 18:36; Luke 17:20-21), and we are reigning right now! We do not teach that 666 is a physical mark but rather a spiritual witness. We do not teach that Jesus will return to earth and set up a throne on earth for 1000 years, but rather, he has established his rule in the hearts and lives of His people right now and is reigning now with power over sin and death. We do not teach a future battle on horseback called Armageddon; but rather, we are in the battle for good and evil now as we live the word and will of God daily in a broken and lost world.
