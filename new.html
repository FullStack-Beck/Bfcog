What to Expect

Your first Sunday

A few things to know before you visit — nothing to prepare, just come as you are.

I.

Come as you are

There's no dress code here — jeans and a t-shirt are just as welcome as Sunday best.

II.

You won't be singled out

Visitors are welcome to simply sit back and take it in — no pressure to stand, speak, or be introduced.

III.

Kids are welcome

Bring the whole family — check-in and children's ministry are ready for you when you arrive.

IV.

Say hello afterward

Stick around after the service — our welcome team would love to meet you and answer questions.
